Fantom Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Fantom is a team messaging app built with privacy in mind. We limit data collection to what is needed to operate the service.

Information We Collect

  • Account information (name, email, department)
  • Messages and attachments you send within the app
  • Basic app usage data (for service reliability)

Privacy Features

  • End-to-end encryption: messages are encrypted so only you and the recipient can read them.
  • Invisible chat: ephemeral sessions where nothing is stored — content disappears when you leave.
  • Disappearing messages: messages can be set to auto-delete after a chosen time.
  • App lock: protect the app with a PIN.

How We Use Data

  • Provide messaging features and file sharing
  • Maintain account access and security
  • Improve app reliability and performance

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share data with service providers required to operate the app.

Data Retention

Data is stored only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law. Invisible chats are never stored, and disappearing messages and attachments are deleted automatically after their retention period.

Your Choices

You can request updates or deletion of your account data by contacting support.
